REVIVAL ZROREVIVAL ZROREVIVAL ZRO
01 / main project

An infinite Hell — a reality-warping dimension that generates personalized nightmares for every soul trapped within. At its deepest point, monsters spontaneously emerge and begin their ascent, threatening every layer above.

A cult revived three ancient mages. A sentient fungus took control of their bodies and powers. Three portals were torn open. An endless tide of monsters now floods Limbo — the neutral zone between Hell and the living world.

Four unlikely heroes must fight back. Demon twins, a battle angel, and a witch with a hidden agenda.

Setting

Limbo is the primary battlefield — a decaying region where gothic cathedrals stand next to brutalist bunkers, cartoon forests grow between ancient ruins. Hell itself shifts art styles based on whose nightmare is being generated. No two souls experience the same Hell.

Tone

SillyFunnyCool

Rule of Cool above all. No stat grinding — progression through skill and style. Combat is the most important feature. Everything else supports it.
